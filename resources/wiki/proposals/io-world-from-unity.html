13. io-world’s Unity levels, made again

Status: proposed, after 9 to 12 · Author’s approval: proposal ☐ plan ☐

Why

io-world began in Unity, in December 2022. Its scene Demoivania had more than the Godot level proposal 7 made again: a menu with music and lights, a long level with levels 2 and 3 started, buttons, a cube, rails, magnets, liquids and a second character. They went away when the game moved to Godot.

Made again in Go Engine, they are the next test of the engine. They need prefabs (proposal 9), richer tile maps (10), sprites made of tiles (11) and sound (12). And they bring two pieces of their own.

What

Id Requirement Check
R1 A liquid: a trigger that slows what moves in it and lets it float up tests a body slows in a liquid, a light body floats up in a liquid
R2 Two characters: a behaviour hands control from one to the other, and the camera follows the one in control test the camera follows the character in control
R3 io-world’s start menu, with its music and lights, opens the game capture on the 3DS
R4 The long level, with levels 2 and 3 as far as Unity had them, plays on the 3DS the author on the console

Out of scope

  • What Unity’s version never finished: levels 2 and 3 end where they ended.
  • Changing io-world’s design. Its open choices are written down, not decided here.

Design

Liquids (physics): a trigger gains a drag, a share of speed lost each second, and a lift, a pull upwards, the opposite of gravity. Both are settings beside pull (proposal 7), so the inspector shows them with it.

Two characters live in the game, not the engine: a Swap behaviour moves the camera’s follow and which node reads the input. The engine only needs follow to change while playing, which the test of R2 checks.

io-world: a script reads Demoivania.unity from io-world’s history once, as proposal 7’s did for Godot. Its pieces become placed scenes (9), its bricks a tileset (10), and its music Ogg (12).
