🧭 Level 1: a forest of your own

You make the game’s project, a 2D scene, and the forest behind everything: hills, trees and a sky. At the end you can play it, even though nothing moves yet.

The idea

A 2D scene is a stage seen from the front. Its pictures are flat cut-outs standing at different depths: the hills at the back, the trees in front of them, later the slime in front of all. The camera is the window the player looks through.

The project

go-engine new ~/games/forest "Forest"

The editor opens on a 3D scene with a cube. This game is 2D, so it needs pictures. Take the forest’s from the engine’s own example:

git clone --depth 1 https://github.com/akrck02/go-engine.git /tmp/go-engine
cp -r /tmp/go-engine/examples/platformer/assets/sprites ~/games/forest/assets/

In Project (key 1) the folder sprites appears, with the pictures: the forest’s tiles, its backdrops, a slime, a coin, spikes and a sign.

A 2D scene

  1. Click the scene’s name at the top (main) and choose New 2D scene. It opens with a green ground, a square and a camera.
  2. Choose the Square in the tree on the left and delete it with the bin above the tree.
  3. Click an empty part of the view to choose nothing: the inspector shows the Scene. Name it Forest, make the sky #11adc1, and set Pixels per metre to 16. The forest’s tiles are 16 pixels across, so each tile will be a metre.

The backdrop

  1. In Project, open sprites and choose background-far.png. Press Place in the scene.
  2. The editor goes back to Level, where the picture is a node named after it. Rename it Hills, and set its Draw order to 0.1.
  3. Do the same with background-near.png: name it Trees, draw order 0.2. A greater draw order is drawn in front.
  4. Drag both so they sit together behind the ground.

The camera

Choose the Camera. Its inspector says how many metres it sees: set Metres seen, top to bottom to 9.4 and across to 15.5. Drag it over the hills; the white frame is what the player will see.

Last, tell the game to start here. In Project, press Settings and choose this scene as The scene the game starts with. Save with Ctrl+S.

Checkpoint

Press Play (key 5).

You see the hills, the trees and the green ground through the camera. Press 3DS above the view to see it at the console’s shape.

Bonus: a dusk sky

Make the sky the deep blue of a late evening.

🔓 Show the solution Choose nothing, then in the scene's inspector set the sky to `#1d3557`. The pictures stay the same; only the sky behind them changes. In level 5 the whole forest goes dark, with lights.

You made a project, a 2D scene and a camera.
