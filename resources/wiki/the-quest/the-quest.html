🗺️ The quest: a forest platformer

In six levels you make one game, a step at a time. A slime walks and jumps through a forest. It collects coins, avoids the water and reaches a sign at the end, at night, under lamps. Then you play it on a Nintendo 3DS. Each level adds a little to the game before it, and ends with something you can play.

Before you start, follow Get started: Go Engine installed and a first project played. Each level takes fifteen to twenty minutes.

Every level has the same shape:

  • the idea, in everyday words;
  • the steps, in the editor and in code;
  • a checkpoint, to know it works;
  • a bonus, with its solution folded;
  • an achievement to unlock.
Level You learn You end with Achievement
1. A forest of your own 2D scenes, pictures, the camera A forest you can look at 🧭 Explorer
2. A slime that walks Sprites, bodies, your first behaviour A slime you move with the arrows 🎭 Puppeteer
3. Ground to stand on Tile maps, solid tiles, a camera that follows A level to run and jump through 🧱 Builder
4. Coins and water Triggers, Touched, changing scenes A level you can win and lose 🪙 Collector
5. Night in the forest The ambient, point lights, sprite lights The forest at night, lit by lamps 🏮 Lamplighter
6. In your pocket Building for a computer, the 3DS and Android Your game on a console 🎒 Traveller
