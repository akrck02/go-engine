🧱 Level 3: ground to stand on

The green box was a stand-in. Now you paint a real level with the forest’s tiles, ledges to jump between, and make the camera follow the slime through it.

The idea

A tile map is a grid, like squared paper, and a tileset is a box of stamps: small square pictures of grass, rock and earth. Painting the level is stamping tiles into squares. Some stamps are solid: the physics turns each row of solid tiles into ground, so you never draw a body for the floor by hand.

The tileset

In Project, choose forest.tileset.json and press Open in Art. It cuts the forest’s picture into tiles of 16 pixels. The tiles tinted blue are solid: grass on top, rock and earth below. Click a tile on the picture to make bodies stand on it; click it again to let them through.

The map

  1. In Level, delete the green box, Ground: the tiles will be the ground now.
  2. Press + and choose Tile map. Name it Ground.
  3. In its inspector, choose the tileset assets/sprites/forest.tileset.json.
  4. Set the cells: 14 across, 8 down. The map’s top-left corner is the node’s position. Keep the slime above where you will paint.

Painting

The palette under the view shows the tileset. Choose a tile in it, then a tool on the left of the view:

Tool Key What it does
Paint B Stamps the tile where you click or drag
Erase E Empties the cells you click or drag
Fill G Fills the empty cells joined to the one you click
Select V Back to choosing and dragging nodes

Paint a long ledge for the slime to start on, then two or three smaller ones, each a jump away. Bodies, over the view, fills the solid tiles in blue: that is where the slime will stand.

A camera that follows

Choose the Camera. Under Follows, choose Player, and set the smoothing to 0.2 seconds: the camera catches up gently instead of jerking. Leave Its view keeps inside on The tile maps, so the player never sees past the level’s edges.

Checkpoint

Press Play and run from ledge to ledge.

The camera follows the slime, and stops where the tiles end. If the slime falls through a ledge, check in Art that its tiles are solid.

Bonus: a gap that matters

Leave a gap two tiles wide between two ledges, and make the slime need a run-up to cross it.

🔓 Show the solution Erase (E) two cells of a ledge. At a speed of 3.2 metres a second and a jump of 18.75, the slime clears two metres easily; to make it hard, set its **speed** to 2.5 in the inspector. The level's difficulty lives in the scene's numbers, so try them in Play and change them without touching the code.

🧱 Achievement unlocked: Builder. You painted a level and made the camera follow your hero.
