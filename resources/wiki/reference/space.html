space

import "github.com/akrck02/go-engine/space"

Package space is where things are: positions, directions, rotations and sizes in a game’s world. Its Transform places a thing: where it is, how it is turned, and how large it is.

The world follows glTF and go-renderer. One unit is one metre and Y points up. The axes are right-handed: with X to the right and Y up, Z points towards whoever looks. A camera with no rotation looks along -Z (Forward). Angles are in degrees wherever a person reads or types them. A positive turn is counter-clockwise, looking down the axis towards the origin: a positive turn around Up takes Right (+X) towards Forward (-Z).

Angles

Angles are a rotation as three angles in degrees, the way an inspector shows them: X tilts (pitch), Y turns (yaw) and Z rolls. They apply roll first, then pitch, then yaw, so the yaw is always around the world’s vertical, as a character or a camera turns.

Rotation

Rotation is how a thing is turned. Make one with RotationAround, Angles.Rotation or RotationLookingAt; the zero value is not a rotation, NoRotation is. Inside, it is a unit quaternion: a real part and three imaginary ones, which turn vectors without the gimbal lock of angles and blend smoothly from one to another.
