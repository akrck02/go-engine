💡 Lights

Light a scene, in 2D or 3D: an ambient for everything, lamps where they shine, and in 2D, pictures that glow. To see it done step by step, follow level 5 of the quest.

A lamp

Press + and choose Light, or choose any node and press Make it a light in its inspector. A point light shines all around its node: full within its inner radius, fading to nothing at its outer one, in metres. Its intensity is how strong it is: 1 a plain lamp, up to 2. It moves with its node, and grows with its scale.

The ambient

Choose nothing, and in the scene’s inspector tick An ambient light of its own:

2D 3D
What it is The light that reaches everything, in a colour and an intensity How strong the sky’s light is
None Daylight when the scene has lights, no lighting at all when it has none The sky lights as it is

White at 1 leaves the colours as they are; a dark blue at 0.4 is a night.

A picture that glows (2D)

A sprite light shines through its node’s picture, in its place, and the picture itself is not drawn: a window, a sign, a coin’s gleam. Put it on a child of the node it lights, with the same sprite, so the node keeps its picture and gains the glow.

In the editor

  • Lights, over the 2D view, draws each light’s two circles; in 3D, a chosen lamp shows two rings.
  • Lit, over the 2D view, shows the scene lit as the game will be, or its colours unlit.

From code

A behaviour can change a light while the game plays:

func (alarm *Alarm) Update(context *engine.Context) {
	light, found := context.Node.Light()
	if !found {
		return
	}
	alarm.strength = 1 + math.Sin(context.Time*6) // a field of Alarm: the light keeps pointing at it
	light.Intensity = &alarm.strength
}

How much it costs

Lights cost nothing in a scene that has none. In 2D, all of a frame’s lights are drawn into one picture at half the screen’s resolution, then laid over the scene, so many lights cost little. In 3D, each thing drawn takes the four nearest lamps that reach it. On the 3DS, 3D lamps light at the vertices: a floor shows a lamp’s pool only where it has vertices.
