# 🗺️ The quest: a forest platformer

In six levels you make one game, a step at a time. A slime walks and jumps through a forest. It
collects coins, avoids the water and reaches a sign at the end, at night, under lamps. Then you play
it on a Nintendo 3DS. Each level adds a little to the game before it, and ends with something you
can play.

<p align="center"><img alt="The forest at night on a 3DS emulator: a warm lamp over a sign at the left, two glowing coins, the slime on a long ledge at the right under a blue moonlight" src="../images/quest/3ds.png" width="560"></p>

**Before you start**, follow [Get started](../2-getting-started.md): Go Engine installed and a first
project played. Each level takes fifteen to twenty minutes.

Every level has the same shape:

- **the idea**, in everyday words;
- **the steps**, in the editor and in code;
- a **checkpoint**, to know it works;
- a **bonus**, with its solution folded;
- an **achievement** to unlock.

| Level | You learn | You end with | Achievement |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1. A forest of your own](1-a-forest-of-your-own.md) | 2D scenes, pictures, the camera | A forest you can look at | 🧭 Explorer |
| [2. A slime that walks](2-a-slime-that-walks.md) | Sprites, bodies, your first behaviour | A slime you move with the arrows | 🎭 Puppeteer |
| [3. Ground to stand on](3-ground-to-stand-on.md) | Tile maps, solid tiles, a camera that follows | A level to run and jump through | 🧱 Builder |
| [4. Coins and water](4-coins-and-water.md) | Triggers, `Touched`, changing scenes | A level you can win and lose | 🪙 Collector |
| [5. Night in the forest](5-night-in-the-forest.md) | The ambient, point lights, sprite lights | The forest at night, lit by lamps | 🏮 Lamplighter |
| [6. In your pocket](6-in-your-pocket.md) | Building for a computer, the 3DS and Android | Your game on a console | 🎒 Traveller |

A **final challenge** waits at the end of level 6: a lift that carries the slime up a cliff.
